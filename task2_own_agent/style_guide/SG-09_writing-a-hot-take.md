# SG-09: Writing a hot take

Status: ACTIVE
Category: Content

A hot take is one provocative sentence about AI or the future of software engineering.

- Maximum length: 140 characters.
- It SHOULD be an opinion, not a fact.
- It SHOULD be something other people could disagree with.
- Examples: "Code review will be the last job humans do in software." or "Agents write code like interns: fast, confident, and slightly wrong."

The hot take is shown in a badge on the member card, see the hot take badge document.
