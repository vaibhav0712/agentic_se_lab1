# SG-06: Tone of voice

Status: ACTIVE
Category: Content

Team websites are friendly, curious and a bit self-ironic.

- Write like you are explaining your team to other students at a coffee break.
- Prefer short sentences and active voice.
- Jokes are welcome, see the humor guidelines. Never make fun of other people or groups.
- Avoid marketing language like "revolutionary" or "game-changing", unless you are clearly joking.
- Opinions about AI SHOULD be honest. It is fine to be sceptical.
