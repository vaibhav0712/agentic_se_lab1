# SG-07: Humor guidelines

Status: ACTIVE
Category: Content

Funny is good. Mean is not.

## Do
- Make fun of yourselves, your code, your deadlines and your coffee consumption.
- Use running gags across member cards (for example everyone's "most used git command").
- Exaggerate your fear or love of AI agents.

## Don't
- No jokes about other students, lecturers or groups.
- No offensive stereotypes.
- No jokes that only work in one language without explanation.

Humor MUST NOT make the page harder to use. A joke button that hides the content is not allowed.
