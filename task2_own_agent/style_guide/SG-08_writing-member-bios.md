# SG-08: Writing member bios

Status: ACTIVE
Category: Content

Each member card contains a short bio.

- Length: 60 to 120 words.
- Written in the third person ("Anna studies ..."), not the first person.
- Mention: study background, one or two interests, one fun fact.
- Mention one thing the member thinks about AI in software engineering today.
- Don't include private data such as phone numbers, addresses or birthdays.
