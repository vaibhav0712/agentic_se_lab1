## What I think about AI in software engineering today
AI coding assistants are fantastic accelerators for writing code, drafting initial test cases, and quickly parsing documentation. However, they frequently lack holistic system-level context and can quietly introduce subtle logic flaws or security vulnerabilities. Developers must remain the critical gatekeepers, actively reviewing and validating every line generated rather than blindly trusting the output.

## Where software engineering will be in 5–10 years
Software engineering will shift from writing raw code line-by-line to orchestrating autonomous, multi-agent systems that handle end-to-end design, implementation, and deployment workflows. Developers will transition into high-level system architects and security auditors who define guardrails, constraints, and intent while AI agents manage iterative implementation. Cross-domain expertise; especially in security, reliability, and edge-case validation, will become even more critical as the volume of generated code scales exponentially.
