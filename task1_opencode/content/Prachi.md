## What I think about AI in software engineering today
<!-- 2-4 sentences. Honest, not marketing. -->
AI tools help a lot with small jobs, like writing simple code and tests. But they can also write code that looks right and is still wrong. So we still need people who can read the code and find mistakes. This is very important in security work.

## Where software engineering will be in 5-10 years
<!-- 2-4 sentences -->
People will write less code by hand. They will spend more time checking and testing what AI writes. AI will do the easy parts. People will focus on design, safety, and finding bugs.

## My hot take on AI
<!-- one provocative sentence, max 140 characters -->
AI will not take your job. But someone who can check AI code well might.
